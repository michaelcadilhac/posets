For an actual-contains trace, build constructs from recorded before generators.
The recorded query then runs, and rebuild is a second fresh constructor from the
same before generators while the first region and query remain alive.

For a CPre trace, build constructs from before generators. The whole-CPre timer
covers transformations/union/intersection and temporary destruction. Result
verification and the initial-state membership check happen outside that timer.
Rebuild then freshly constructs from recorded AFTER generators, while the
computed frontier and verification scaffolding remain alive. Thus its input
cardinality can differ from the trace's before/frontier cardinality.

Neither replay rebuild field measures an in-place KD rebuild or isolated lazy
maintenance. Allocation-profiler rebuild counters are separate quantities.
The choice-freeze wording 'second fresh construction with first instance alive'
is a high-level category; these per-kind details govern reporting. When exporting
tables, label the trace descriptor as before_maxima and supply after_maxima or
construction cardinality where available, especially for CPre rebuild rows.
