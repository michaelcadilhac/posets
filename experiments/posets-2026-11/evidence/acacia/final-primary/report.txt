All 15 arms completed the same 1,524 SYNTCOMP26 cases: 22,860 runs. This is the finished first campaign. The subsequent focused default-backend optimization is not included in these binaries or scores.

Current decision: keep the existing ranked-vector default. Lazy FC is the closest alternative: it solves the identical 1,369 cases and has a PAR2 total 4.283s (0.072%) lower. This was one unrepeated full campaign; no noise floor or statistical advantage was established. Every other alternative has zero gains and one to four losses against this default.

Protocol: unchanged shipping preset otf_sparse_formula_parity, empty flags, strict measured wall time <=17 seconds, 8 GiB RAM/no swap, serial case execution, batch32/seed260617 with balanced arm order, no CPU affinity or quota.
