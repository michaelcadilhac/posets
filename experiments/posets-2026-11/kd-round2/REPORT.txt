The useful change retained from this round is a small optimization to the existing
KD tree. A normal dominance query now restores each temporary bound as recursion
returns, so it no longer clears an array across every dimension before each query.
Exception recovery still clears scratch state before rethrowing. The reviewed
change and its tests are included in PR 40 at commit d363b9f. Acacia backend selection remains ranked-vector.

The lazy KD maintenance and leaf-filter experiments did not establish a broad
CPre improvement and are excluded from production. Their patches, unfavorable
measurements and censors remain in this package. The exact offset-only catalog
control was also rejected after its separate measurement; its result is not
inferred from the combined packed-catalog experiment.

The software work and heap pass helps explain why the filter did not help this
workload. Across the captured larger round-robin CPre, the four profiled lazy variants
made 10,149,479 full partial-order attempts. The old and optimized ranked controls
made 5,376,016 and 5,273,875 attempts on that same frame. This is a measured work-count
gap; it does not identify a cache cause. For the isolated captured miss, every one
of the 4,067 frozen candidates passed the selected columns, plus 521
pending comparisons. The filter rejected nothing on that query. It did execute
227 explicit SIMD chunks before an explicit rebuild; after that rebuild, balanced
leaves held only 8 or 9 points and no complete 16-point chunk was available. A
zero chunk count after rebuild does not mean SIMD never ran earlier. Neither
observation justifies changing the lane cap or choosing another axis after seeing
these results, and no such follow-up was performed.

Compact metadata did reduce construction work. On the quoted round-robin frontier,
allocation requests fell from 965 to 703, and extra tracked construction heap fell
from 590,748 to 535,350 bytes. On the full-arbiter frontier they fell from 1,045 to
759 requests and from 808,232 to 722,764 extra bytes. That is about 27% fewer
allocation requests and 9–11% less extra tracked construction heap. It was not a
whole-CPre timing win. Two-column filters lowered some constructor-only order
counts but added retained columns and did not lower the round-robin CPre count.

Heap figures have explicit limits. Extra tracked heap is measured above region
entry after caller Vector materialization. Known-retained values use capacities,
include enabled diagnostic statistics, and exclude opaque Vector heap payloads;
they are neither shipping sizeof values nor total backend heap. The tracker covers
C++ allocation requests and excludes direct malloc/mmap, stacks and hook overhead.
Index counters after construction or CPre describe the surviving index epoch, not
all discarded epochs. Peaks are not summed into a whole-operation peak. This round
collected no PMU or cache-miss measurements, and instrumented times are not clean
performance samples.

All these frames were previously seen TRAIN data. The existing replay kernels,
scalar and SIMD vector types, three external pairs and fixed seeds were retained.
The pairs ran in A then B, B then A, and A then B order. The source oracle had a
30 second cap; checks and measurements each had a 2 second cap. Work ran through

cell support an engineering decision on these frames, not a significance claim
