Deadline checks and final Acacia comparison

Acacia backend selection remains ranked-vector. The final full-corpus comparison
and its longer conditional follow-up solve exactly the same cases with original
ranked vectors and retained lazy-FC KD. Their small timing differences do not
establish a reliable winner. No production optimization is reverted on this
evidence, and this sprint ends with PR40; further KD work is a separate task.

Full comparison and conditional follow-up

The genuine original control is Acacia 86dd487 with bundled Posets e167. The
candidate is the genuine frozen cc058ca lazy-FC executable, whose lazy algorithm
matches the retained implementation. It is not relabelled as a freshly rebuilt
current binary. Both use the shipping otf_sparse_formula_parity portfolio,
empty runtime flags, 16 signed-char SIMD lanes, 8 GiB cgroups and no swap.

At 17 seconds, each solves 1,368 of 1,524 cases: 629 realizable and 739
unrealizable, with the same 156 timeouts. There are no gains or losses.
Total PAR2 is 5,946.655226 seconds for original ranked vectors and 5,940.166345
for lazy-FC, a difference of -6.488881 seconds (about -0.1091%). Each unsolved
case costs 34 seconds. This is one complete campaign, not a significance claim.
All 3,048 native/resource rows are present, with no errors, contradictory
answers, decisive wall overruns or OOM kills. Maximum reaped-process RSS is
3,976,986,624 versus 3,977,768,960 bytes. Whole-cgroup peaks are separately
4,239,486,976 versus 4,243,906,560 bytes; neither measures retained backend heap.

The strict 17-second timeout union contains 156 cases. Both unchanged frozen
arms receive one 30-second primary attempt per case. Each resolves the same
eight cases (five realizable and three unrealizable), retains 146 native
TIMEOUTs, and has two native ERRORs from Spot's acceptance-set limit. There
are no gains or losses. Conditional PAR2 is 9,054.294432966038586 seconds for
the original and 9,054.435914172790946 for lazy-FC; every unsolved case,
including ERROR, costs 60 seconds. All 312 resource rows are valid, with no
OOM events, truth conflicts or decisive overruns. This is a conditional cohort
of previously seen cases, not a new 1,524-case run at 30 seconds. The 17-second
observations and scores are unchanged.

At 30 seconds, peak process RSS is 4,082.121094 versus 4,082.015625 MiB.
Whole-cgroup peaks are separately 5,851.957031 versus 5,879.597656 MiB.
There is no meaningful observed process-RSS advantage for either configuration.

The first native ERROR stopped execution after 205 primary observations.
A separate diagnostic reproduced the original e167 arm's exit 3 on
ltl2dba_R_pb_12_pe_.ltl. Its complete stderr contains an outside-proved-DPA-
Buchi status message, three identical Spot exceptions saying the acceptance-set
limit is 32, and final ERROR. The original primary stderr was not retained;
that one observation is explicitly adjudicated from its bound primary record
and the separate diagnostic, not from recovered original text. The diagnostic
is not scored and never replaces the primary time, result, exit or resources.

A reviewed continuation preserved all 205 records and executed only the
remaining 107 original visits in order. A transparent adapter called the
unchanged native scoped runner with identical arguments and returned the same
RunResult object, writing its existing full stdout/stderr only after the
measured/resource interval. No route, phase, worker or deadline option was
added. Three later primary ERRORs have their own exact captured signature.
All four remain native ERROR with separate SPOT_ACCEPTANCE_LIMIT_32 classification
and unsolved cost 60. Unknown, incomplete or mixed errors would stop. One pair
spans the interruption: 102 uninterrupted prefix pairs, one split pair, and
53 uninterrupted suffix pairs. We do not claim all 156 pairs were uninterrupted
adjacent calls, or infer a paired speed ratio for the ERROR pair. Summary-only
replay was byte-identical and launched no solver calls; 215 final invariance
bindings matched. The original-checkout proof reports all five aliases and all
six fields matching, with ignored/untracked bytes explicitly outside its scope.

Deadline repeat and matched attribution

The preceding deadline check repeated 45 historical pair/case cells five times,
for 450 calls on six deliberately selected, already-seen cases. There were
219 decisive answers and 231 timeouts, with no errors, truth conflicts, decisive
overruns or OOM kills. Every resource observation was present. Only four cells
were fully uncensored. The engineering screen retained 27 serious solve-loss
and two timing-loss signals, but most compare different backends; these are not

The previous ranked implementation solved load_balancer_unreal1_pb_6_4_pe_.ltl
in 4/5 attempts and the optimized default in 5/5. Both solved robot_repair5 in

The historical binaries retain their original source and dependency contexts.

One matched control isolates regular-sharingtree memoization on
Alarm_a5f99bc6.ltl. Both builds share exact common objects, static and runtime

Scope and limits

The earlier three-arm KD package's 789 baseline is the previous ranked
implementation, despite its historical "Original ranked default" display label.
The genuine original control here is e167. No old package or data is rewritten,
and the earlier 15-arm and three-arm campaigns are not pooled with this work.

Full Acacia runs use compatible 16-lane, 128-bit Posets SIMD despite -march=native.
Acacia explicitly sets SIMD_IS_NATIVE=false and SIMD_IS_MAX=false; Posets alone
defaults to native width without overrides. Separate three-repetition TRAIN
replay medians at 64 lanes were mixed: on round_robin_arbiter_pb_4_pe_.ltl,
lazy CPre was 180.500268 versus 137.109857 ms and ranked CPre 98.552219 versus
77.895543 ms; full-arbiter frontier construction was 84.582781 versus 99.318678 ms.
These are not a dedicated paired width experiment or native-width full Acacia
scores. See Acacia issue 380 for deferred work.

The shipping portfolio has seven competing workers. Four native workers do not
use the selected downset, the sparse worker uses its own search structures, and
forward search uses the selected downset chiefly for certificate checking or
backward fallback. Translation and several direct checks precede downset use.
Within backward CPre, apply and nontrivial intersection create fresh downsets,
limiting index amortization without eliminating reuse within an index epoch.
Primary route/phase/worker recording was off and NO_VERBOSE removed verbose
messages. Thus no measured winning-route frequency or bottleneck percentage is
claimed. The routing note supplies exact source conditions. Separate recorded
CPre work counts are not full-corpus route shares.

The original and lazy configurations also contain different private Rust archive
bytes. These are whole-configuration results, not isolated causal measurements
of a Posets source change. No cache/PMU mechanism or fixed-clock claim follows.
Kernel OOM observations are reported separately from native TIMEOUT/MEMOUT/ERROR;
there are zero OOM-kill-positive invocations in all four stages here.
