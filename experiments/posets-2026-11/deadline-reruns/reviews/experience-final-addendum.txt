Final review experience: the 30-second campaign

Both the Nbjorner and Barbosa reviewers independently found that the original
follow-up controller could accept an ERROR outcome without stopping. That shared
finding mattered in practice: the corrected controller stopped on the original
default's first native ERROR, preserving the result before any continuation.

A separate bounded diagnostic reproduced Spot's acceptance-set limit. The original
stderr had not been retained, so it was not presented as recovered evidence. Both
reviewers checked the continuation rules and then independently recomputed the
completed cohort. The original ERROR remained ERROR; the diagnostic was excluded
from scoring; three later errors had exact captured signatures; and one pair's
interruption was disclosed. The final audits accepted all 312 primary observations.

This adds a concrete example to the earlier experience ledger: Barbosa supplied
another adversarial check of failure handling and the evidence needed to justify
continuation. Nbjorner supplied an independent check of the same gap, plus source,
identity and arithmetic audits. Credit for the error guard is shared. The earlier
allocation-failure and control-fixture findings remain recorded in the v1 addendum.

These were the same GPT-6.1 Sol model at xhigh, using different skills and sometimes
different assignments. This was not a controlled comparison of reviewer quality or
cost, and the researchers themselves did not participate. The evidence supports
keeping the complementary review roles; it does not establish that either skill
is universally better.
